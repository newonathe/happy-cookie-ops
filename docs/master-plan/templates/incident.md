# Incident record

- Incident ID / severity / owner:
- Started / detected / acknowledged / contained / recovered / closed (UTC):
- Affected release/schema/device/event/period:
- Business impact: lost/unavailable/exposed records, cents/quantity uncertainty:
- Verified facts and evidence hashes:
- Unknowns; external actions with unknown outcome:
- Containment and manual fallback:
- Backup/queue/source records preserved:
- Privacy notification applicability assessment and responsible owner:
- Recovery actions and reconciliation totals:
- Newer writes and duplicate/replay checks:
- Owner decision to resume:
- Root/contributing causes, supported by evidence:
- Corrective tasks, owners and deadlines:
- Test/monitor/runbook improvements:
- Follow-up review date:

Do not paste PII, secrets or full payment proofs. Preserve the evidence privately with access control. Do not use incident response as authorization to erase data or bypass a user's permission gate.
